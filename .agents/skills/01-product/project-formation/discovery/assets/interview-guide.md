# Discovery Interview Guide

Use this guide to gather high-quality evidence.

## Interview Principles

- Ask about recent real behavior, not opinions about hypothetical futures.
- Probe for frequency, impact, and current workaround cost.
- Separate user pain from internal preference.

## High-Signal Questions

- "Tell me about the last time this problem happened."
- "What did you do to solve it in that moment?"
- "What was the cost of that workaround in time, money, or risk?"
- "How often does this happen and for whom?"
- "What would make you switch behavior?"

## Evidence Quality Scale

- weak: anecdotal, indirect, or stale evidence.
- moderate: multiple user signals, partial quantification.
- strong: recent direct signals with clear pattern and quantified impact.
