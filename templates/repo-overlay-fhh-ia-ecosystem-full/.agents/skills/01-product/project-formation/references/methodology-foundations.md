# Methodology Foundations

This workflow integrates clean-room interpretations from publicly available sources.

## Core Methods

- Shape Up: appetite-first betting, fixed time/variable scope, explicit rabbit holes and no-gos.
- Continuous discovery: ongoing user evidence and assumption testing before scaling investment.
- Agile roadmapping: outcome-linked Now/Next/Later sequencing and regular roadmap refresh.
- GTM experimentation: hypothesis-driven launch design with measurable thresholds.

## Source Map (for conceptual grounding)

- Basecamp Shape Up (book site): betting, appetite, shaping, risk framing.
- SVPG articles: product discovery uncertainty, avoid time-boxed fake discovery, build-to-learn mindset.
- Atlassian product roadmapping guidance: audience-specific roadmap detail, update cadence, anti-patterns.
- GOV.UK service manual (discovery/alpha framing): evidence quality and staged confidence progression.

## Usage Notes

- Do not copy external text verbatim into generated deliverables.
- Translate principles into context-specific reasoning and decisions.
- Always log source URL and retrieval date when web research affects a recommendation.
