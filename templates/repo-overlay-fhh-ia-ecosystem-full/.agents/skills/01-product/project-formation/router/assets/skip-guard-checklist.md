# Skip Guard Checklist

Apply before any stage transition.

## Checklist

- Do we have enough evidence to justify the transition?
- Are top assumptions explicit and testable?
- Are key terms, scope boundaries, and success criteria unambiguous?
- Is the proposed scope bounded to appetite?
- Are risks visible with at least one control each?
- Could we defend this transition to engineering and leadership?

## Verdict

- pass: all checks are yes.
- hold: one or more checks are no or uncertain.

## If Hold

Ask one targeted question that most improves decision quality and remain in the current stage.

If ambiguity is the main blocker, ask a disambiguation question first and postpone stage transition.
