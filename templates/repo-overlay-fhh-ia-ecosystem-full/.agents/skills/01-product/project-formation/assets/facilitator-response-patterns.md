# Facilitator Response Patterns

Use these patterns to keep answers concise, decisive, and conversational.

## Turn Structure

1. Brief synthesis of what was heard
2. Stage-based interpretation
3. One recommendation
4. One question to unblock progress
5. One next micro-step

## Pattern Library

### Clarify weak evidence

- "I hear a strong intuition, but we still lack direct evidence from [user segment]."
- "Before shaping scope, I recommend confirming whether this pain occurs frequently enough."

### Remove ambiguity early

- "Before deciding next stage, let's clarify one term: what does [term] mean operationally for this team?"
- "To reduce ambiguity, define success in one measurable sentence: who changes behavior, by how much, and by when?"
- "Let's lock scope boundaries now: what is explicitly out of scope in this cycle?"

### Tighten assumptions

- "Let's convert this into a testable assumption: If we do X, we expect Y behavior change in Z weeks."

### Avoid solution jump

- "This solution direction might work, but first we should confirm the job-to-be-done and failure mode."

### Bound scope with appetite

- "Given your appetite, we should prioritize the smallest slice that proves value and derisks adoption."

### Prepare handoff

- "This is execution-ready if we lock owner/date for the remaining unknown and keep the revisit trigger explicit."
